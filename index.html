How we work

Built with the people who use it.

We build and support Aangan as our own product, working alongside communities to understand the routines, responsibilities and relationships it needs to serve.

Every community has its own way of doing things. The product should start by understanding that.

We would rather change the software. So before we set anything up, we sit with your committee and learn how your society runs today — the paper register at the gate, the spreadsheet the treasurer keeps. Those are the things the product has to fit around.

  • Start from your process. Not from our module list. What already works stays; we only replace what is genuinely costing someone their evenings.
  • Switch on only what you need. Every module is enabled per community. A society that wants nothing but the community side never sees a billing screen.
  • Keep improving together. A workflow specific to your society, a report your auditor needs — bring us the details so we can understand what the product should do next.
